### <u>NOTE:</u> Account creation required to view the file in the EasyEDA website

- Open the following link: https://pro.easyeda.com/
  
- Click on the "Use Online" button in the home page.
  
- Sign up and create an account OR alternatively sign in with google account.
  
- Now, in the home page of the editor (link as - https://pro.easyeda.com/editor), go to the top ribbon "File > Import > EasyEDA(Professional)".
  
- Open the downloaded "ProPrj_VISHWAKARMA_ROVER.epro2" file.
  
- In the Import window, just click on Import; again in the Import Document window, just click on Import.
  
- In the notice, click on Yes to open the imported project.
  
- In the Project Design tab on the left, double click on "Schematic1" (Schematic1 is under Board1, which is under the main project folder).
  
- Schematic is now visible in the canvas.
